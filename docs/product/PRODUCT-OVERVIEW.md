# Product Overview

## Product

The Adaptive Learning Platform is a secure, scalable vocational-learning and revision platform combining structured teaching with evidence-driven adaptation.

Its primary differentiator is the ability to distinguish different likely root causes behind superficially similar learner errors and provide targeted remediation.

## Initial learner problem

Vocational learners commonly combine course notes, textbooks, YouTube, question banks, mock exams, general-purpose AI and forums. These can help, but often leave the learner to answer: **Why do I keep getting this type of question wrong?**

A wrong Electrical calculation may result from misunderstanding the electrical relationship, weak algebra, reciprocal/fraction arithmetic, unit/prefix conversion, procedural error, strategy limitation or a simple slip. Treating all failures as the same topic weakness wastes time and can leave foundations unresolved.

## Core value proposition

Help learners learn the complete syllabus, revise efficiently, identify genuine weak areas, understand recurring mistakes, fix foundational gaps, prepare for assessments and build transferable understanding.

## Initial market

Primary first market: **UK vocational education**.

First full vertical: **Electrical**.

Initial proving context: **City & Guilds 2365 Level 2 — Unit 202 Principles of Electrical Science**.

The architecture is not restricted to that qualification.

## Foundational domains

### Foundational Maths

Arithmetic, decimals, fractions, reciprocals, ratio/proportion, substitution, formula transposition, powers/indices where needed, scientific/engineering notation, SI prefixes and conversion.

### Foundational Physics

Quantities and units, force, work, energy, power, efficiency, proportionality, heat/temperature, pressure, basic mechanics, materials and magnetism/electromagnetism foundations. Exact coverage is curriculum-driven.

## Product modes

- Learn
- Practise
- Improve Weak Areas
- Quick Revision
- Mock / Exam Practice
- Progress

These share one learner model.

## Learn mode

A learner can select qualification → unit → ordered lesson and work through the complete supported syllabus. Lessons can also be found through foundational-domain views.

## Adaptive diagnosis

Two learners can fail the same Electrical question for different reasons and should receive different support. The platform must not claim false diagnostic certainty.

## Feedback depth

Learners may prefer result only, concise correction, targeted explanation or deep explanation/remediation. The backend can maintain rich evidence while the UI remains concise.

## Initial AI position

Launch does not require an LLM during learner use. AI is used in development/content production. A future premium tutor may provide conversational explanation while deterministic core functionality remains independently available.

## Commercial direction

Initial commercial hypothesis: low-friction B2C subscription; likely monthly/annual options; pricing experimentally validated; free trials and organic outreach initially; later B2B/institutional possibilities. The product must earn expansion through evidence of demand.

## Initial boundaries

Not initially: full LMS, ePortfolio, employer HR system, social network, tutor marketplace, practical-competence certification system, unrestricted AI assistant or proprietary-reference substitute.

## Success

Technical sophistication is not success by itself. The product succeeds when learners genuinely prefer using it because it helps them progress faster, understand weaknesses, learn effectively and prepare confidently for assessment.
