# Source-of-Truth Map (CC-13B)

Full inventory of every plausible authority for each of 8 fact-categories across the learning-package pipeline, compiled from direct code/content reads across all audit threads. Categories with more than one entry are cross-referenced to `DUPLICATE-SOURCE-OF-TRUTH-REGISTER.md` where the multiplicity is a real finding, or noted as a deliberately-separated, non-conflicting split otherwise.

| Authority | Role |

| `packages/content-schema/src/knowledge-graph.ts` | Schema/integrity authority (FK resolution, no duplicate ids, provenance-required-for-approved). |

**Verdict**: one genuine authority (`cc04-unit202-electrical-science.ts`), one explicit derived-audit layer, one inert DB mirror. No conflict found. See `DUPLICATE-SOURCE-OF-TRUTH-REGISTER.md` DUP-3 for the related "curriculum fact represented in 5 places" structural-fragility finding (a cross-category concern, not this category alone).

## e. Visual asset production-eligibility

**Confirmed duplicate source of truth — see `DUPLICATE-SOURCE-OF-TRUTH-REGISTER.md` DUP-1.** At minimum: `unit202-artwork-manifest.json` (documented-authoritative, does not exist in the repo), `tools/visual-production-studio/data/studio-state.json` (workflow status), `reports/instructional-visuals/unit202-canonical-visual-registry.json` (shipment-candidate registry, whose own generator explicitly disclaims reconciling with the manifest above), several further audit/debt-register JSONs, and `apps/mobile/src/components/diagrams/DiagramRenderer.tsx`'s `CANONICAL_ASSET_LOCK` (the one actually consulted at runtime, structurally disconnected from all the others). No single reconciling authority exists.

## f. Lesson-availability/catalogue/release-membership

**Confirmed duplicate source of truth — see `DUPLICATE-SOURCE-OF-TRUTH-REGISTER.md` DUP-2.** `scripts/content/data/content-releases.ts` (`MOBILE_BUNDLED_RELEASE_ID`) is the authored release manifest; `apps/mobile/src/lib/lesson-content/local-content-registry.ts` (`getLocalReleaseLessons`) is the correct runtime reader of it (confirmed: returns `projection.lessons` verbatim, no filtering). But `packages/diagnostic-engine/src/course-definitions.ts` (`UNIT202_ADAPTIVE_VERTICAL`) independently hardcodes its own `contentRelease` field and its own 4-lesson subset — structurally separate, and this specific duplication already caused a real regression once (documented in that file's own header comment: the field drifted to `v2` while the real bundle advanced to `v7`, which would have thrown `UnknownCourseActivityError` for every learner). Currently back in sync at `v8` but not mechanically guaranteed to stay so.

| Authority | Role |

## h. Design-system version/tokens

**Confirmed duplicate, by design, neither final — see `DUPLICATE-SOURCE-OF-TRUTH-REGISTER.md` DUP-4.** `apps/mobile/src/lib/tokens.ts` (RN `StyleSheet` constants) vs. `apps/web/app/globals.css` (Tailwind variables) — materially different palettes, no shared version identifier, no `packages/design-system`. This is UI-chrome tokens, distinct from the separately-tracked instructional-visual design-system version (`ALP-VDS-2026-08-29`, `docs/design/ALP-PRODUCT-WIDE-VISUAL-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`), which governs illustrated artwork, not app chrome, and which itself has zero real asset bindings (see `VISUAL-GOVERNANCE-AND-COVERAGE-REGISTER.md` §5).

## Cross-cutting observation

Every genuine duplication found (d/e/f/h) sits either in the **visual production pipeline** (d, e — two eras of tooling never reconciled) or in **release/version identity** (f, h — a value re-declared in a second place with no shared constant). The curriculum/lesson/question layers (a, b, c, g) are each held together by a single real authored source plus explicit, validator-enforced derivation — the discipline that prevented duplication there was not consistently applied when the visual-production tooling and the release/version identifiers were built. This is the single clearest structural pattern across the whole source-of-truth trace, and should inform how `REMEDIATION-PLAN.md`'s packages are scoped.
