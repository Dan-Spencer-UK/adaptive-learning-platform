## Class III lever

## Motor-effect family
Keep the VFPt source as factual authority but create exact state-specific prepared boards before Gemini. Each board: one N/S pair, one field-line system, one current marker, and (teaching only) one exact force arrow already approved. Do not derive the direction during generation.

## Resistivity
Keep the geometry source as cross-check but prepare internal comparison boards using course notation L and A (`R = ρL/A`). Never expose `S` to Gemini.

## Motional EMF
Use a minimal internal 2D technical board based on the authoritative motional-EMF panel. Do not ask Gemini to convert rails/rod geometry into photoreal apparatus.

## Diode bias
Replace semiconductor-band diagrams as composition authority. Use an exact internal paired circuit reference: identical layout, only source polarity/current state changes.

## Electrolysis
Use an exact internal prepared state containing every ion arrow already correct. Gemini may restyle but may not invent directional micro-semantics.

## Conductor vs insulator
Retire `Stranded lamp wire.jpg` as composition authority. Use a purpose-built cutaway/comparison. A Commons `Conductor vs insulator.jpg` source can be a factual cross-check but must itself pass semantic QA before composition use.
