## Source reuse

**12 unique sources support 16 requirements** — a reuse ratio of 1.33 requirements per source:

- **mathcentre, 'Indices or Powers'** supports both positive indices and negative indices (one document, two requirements).
- **NIST Guide to the SI, Chapter 7** supports both standard/scientific notation and engineering notation (one document, two requirements).
- **NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook, §1.3.5.1 'Measures of Location'** supports mean, median, and mode together (one document, three requirements).
- The remaining 7 new sources (percentages, algebra, Pythagoras, trig ratios, range, decimals, ratios) each support exactly one requirement, where no single existing source covered more than one without stretching scope.
- 2 sources are reused, unchanged, from already-reviewed pilot-002 evidence (fractions; formula transposition).

No requirement's evidence was weakened or generalised merely to enable source reuse; reuse only occurred where a single document genuinely, substantively covered multiple requirements' own required coverage dimensions.

## Depth discipline applied

Two candidates were deliberately rejected for exceeding this qualification's introductory depth ceiling or for insufficient specificity, not for lack of general topical relevance:

- A mathcentre page on the **sine rule and cosine rule** (general, non-right-angled-triangle trigonometry) was retrieved but not used for the sine/cosine/tangent requirement — that requirement is scoped to right-angled-triangle ratios (SOHCAHTOA) only.
- A mathcentre "facts and formulae" ratio reference sheet was judged too brief to substantively teach the procedure and was set aside in favour of the fuller 'Ratios' teach-yourself unit.

The "Algebra" and "Ordinary decimal arithmetic" claims are deliberately scoped to what their sources actually teach (expanding brackets/collecting like terms; place value and powers-of-ten manipulation/decimal-fraction conversion respectively) rather than claimed as exhaustive of "all algebra" or "all decimal arithmetic" — see each result's `scopeNote` in `EVIDENCE-RESULTS.json`.
